Who it's for

This is built for people starting a cleaning agency from zero — no industry experience, no employees, no equipment. The entire model is management-based: you hire self-employed cleaners, book clients, coordinate operations, and keep the margin. You never clean a home yourself.

It is not for people already running an established operation looking for advanced growth tactics, or people who want passive income that requires no work. Building a cleaning agency takes focused effort in the first 60–90 days. After that, with the right systems in place, it runs largely without you.
